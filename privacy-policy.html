Privacy Policy

Effective from: August 5, 2026

This privacy policy describes what personal data the "Finance Manager" app (hereinafter — "the app"), available at mr-bir.github.io/BudzetsIMT and as an Android app, collects and processes, and what rights you have as a user of the app regarding this data.

1. Data Controller

Mārtiņš Barons
Email: birmons@gmail.com

For any questions, requests, or complaints about your data, you can contact us at the email address above.

2. What data we process

2.1 Account data (via Google Sign-In)

When you sign in to the app with a Google account, we receive and store:

  • your email address,
  • your name (if available in your Google account),
  • a unique Google account identifier (UID), which we use to link your data to you alone.

We never see or store your Google account password — sign-in is handled entirely by Google.

2.2 App data you enter yourself

For the app to fulfil its purpose, you enter and the app stores:

  • income/salary amounts,
  • bill and category information (names, amounts, payment status),
  • credit balance information (amounts, terms, monthly payments),
  • your own custom categories and their settings,
  • monthly archive entries.

This data is considered financial information under the GDPR and is processed with heightened care (see Section 5 on security).

2.3 Usage and diagnostic data

We use Firebase Analytics (Google) to understand how the app is used (e.g. how often various sections are opened) and to improve it. This data is generally aggregated and does not contain your financial records.

2.4 Security signals

We use Firebase App Check (with reCAPTCHA Enterprise) to distinguish genuine app requests from automated/malicious access. This process may process technical device/browser signals, but is not intended to identify you.

4. Who we share data with

Data is stored and processed using Google Firebase infrastructure (Firestore database, Authentication, App Check, Analytics). Google acts as our data processor in this context. The Firestore database region is set to Europe (eur3).

We do not sell and never share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. How we protect your data

  • Access to data is controlled by Firestore security rules based on your unique account ID — another user cannot see or modify your data.
  • Firebase App Check restricts access from unrecognized/automated sources.
  • All communication between the app and the servers is encrypted (HTTPS).

6. How long we keep your data

Your data is stored for as long as your account exists in the app. If you request account deletion (see Section 7), all data associated with your account is deleted from our systems within a reasonable time after the request.

7. Your rights and account deletion

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • access your data,
  • correct inaccurate data,
  • request deletion ("the right to be forgotten"),
  • restrict or object to processing,
  • port your data (data portability).
You can request account and data deletion in two ways:
  1. In the app — Settings → "Delete account" (deletes the account and all associated data from Firebase).
  2. Without downloading the app — by emailing birmons@gmail.com.

If you believe your rights have been violated, you have the right to file a complaint with the Data State Inspectorate of Latvia (dvi.gov.lv), or with the data protection authority in your own country.

8. Children's data

The app is not intended for children under 16 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy is materially changed, we will announce it in the app (Settings → "What's new") or by email, where possible.

This privacy policy applies only to the "Finance Manager" (BudzetsIMT) app and does not apply to any other websites or apps by Mārtiņš Barons.
